01About

My work sits where model training meets real products. I post-train large models with SFT and reinforcement learning, build sandboxed environments that turn compilers, GUI checks and visual comparisons into reward, and design evaluations that catch reward hacking before it ships. Lately I have been building self-improving loops: systems that generate their own benchmarks and write verifier feedback back into the agent's skills.

Before Tencent I worked on search relevance at Meituan, multi-agent systems and consumer hardware at Guide Infrared, and on-site industrial software at SUPCON. I studied Electronic and Information Engineering at Imperial College London (MEng, First-Class Honours).
