01About

Early in my career I went looking on purpose. Finance, heavy industry, consumer electronics, internet search: different industries, and a different hat in each. I wanted to see how value is judged, how things get built, and what users actually need, from more than one seat at the table.

AI is where all of those seats turned out to matter at once. I now post-train large models with SFT and reinforcement learning, build sandboxed environments that turn compilers, GUI checks and visual comparisons into reward, and design evaluations that catch reward hacking before it ships. Lately I have been building self-improving loops: systems that generate their own benchmarks and write verifier feedback back into the agent's skills.

Research Engineer, Post-Training · Tencent WeChat, Personal Agent

Post-training 100B+ parameter models for WeChat's personal agent: describe an app in plain language and it builds a working Mini Program, then keeps editing it with you over multiple turns. I also build the persona-driven user agents that test it, and the sandboxed RL environments whose rewards it can't game.

Every earlier dot shows up here: judging value like a banker, respecting constraints like an FDE, starting from the user like a PM.
